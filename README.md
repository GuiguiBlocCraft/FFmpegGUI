# FFmpeg GUI

A very simple **graphic user interface** for **FFmpeg** but __only to cut video__. Possibility to use your graphic card to make render with encoder.

You can cut with **start** and **from** (or with **duration**). And you can add more cuts.

![ffmpeg_gui](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/007378bd-1d69-4bad-a815-5921f9462f96)

## Shortcuts

- `Left arrow` To backward 10 seconds
- `Right arrow` To forward 10 seconds
- `Up arrow` Volume up
- `Down arrow` Volume down
- `Spacebar` Play/Pause video player


## Requirements

- You must to have **ffmpeg** and **ffprobe** in your PATH variable.
- Windows 8 or higher
